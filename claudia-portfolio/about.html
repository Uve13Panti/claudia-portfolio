About…

Hi! I'm a fashion enthusiast originally from Barcelona, Spain. I studied Fashion Design and Fashion Styling in both Barcelona and Milan, where I had the opportunity to develop my creativity, technical skills, and understanding of the fashion industry from an international perspective.

I am currently based in Linköping, Sweden, where I am working while continuing to grow professionally within the fashion industry. I am passionate about fashion, styling, creativity, travelling, and discovering new cultures. I also enjoy learning languages and believe that experiencing different countries and perspectives is a constant source of inspiration for my work.

"My goal is to create authentic, thoughtful, and visually compelling fashion — helping people and brands express their identity through style and design."

Experience

Fashion Design & Styling Intern
The Label Edition

During my internship at The Label Edition, I gained hands-on experience in the fashion industry by supporting the design and production process, assisting with garment development, and collaborating with the creative team. I also worked as a stylist during Barcelona Fashion Week, helping to prepare looks and ensuring every outfit reflected the brand's vision throughout the event.

Fashion Sales Assistant
Stradivarius

Working at Stradivarius strengthened my understanding of customer service, visual merchandising, and the commercial side of the fashion industry. It also enhanced my communication skills and my ability to identify customers' needs while maintaining the brand's image.

Fashion Designer
Independent projects

I have experience designing uniform collections for hotels and hospitality businesses, creating garments that combine functionality, comfort, and a strong visual identity. Throughout my work, I have also participated in different fashion projects, developing concepts, technical drawings, garment specifications, and collections from the initial idea to the final design.
